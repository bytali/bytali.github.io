Our story

A few chapters
before “I do.”

The story isn’t perfect, but it is very us: long conversations, missed exits, family lunches, shared playlists, and one very certain yes.

01 · Hello
June 2019 · Makati

One coffee became four hours.

We met through friends and planned a quick coffee after work. The café closed before we ran out of things to say.

02 · Us
2020–2024 · Everywhere

Love in the everyday things.

Weekend drives, Sunday lunches, surviving traffic, meeting each other’s families, and showing up through every season.

03 · Yes
November 2025 · Tagaytay

The easiest yes.

With a view, shaky hands, and a tiny box Miguel almost dropped, Sofia said yes before he finished the speech.

“Home is wherever we get to come back to each other.”

Dress code

Modern Filipino formal.

Barong Tagalog, Filipiniana-inspired pieces, suits, long dresses, and elevated formalwear are all welcome. Think romantic garden tones with a polished Manila-evening feel.

A gentle style note

We’d love our photos to feel cohesive, so guests are encouraged to wear muted greens, champagne, clay, warm neutrals, and soft earth tones.

  • Please reserve white and ivory for the bride.
  • Church-friendly attire is appreciated for the ceremony.
  • Bring a light layer—reception halls can get chilly.

Good to know

Little details

May we bring our children?

We love your little ones. Because of venue capacity, however, we can only accommodate children specifically named on the invitation.

Can we bring a plus-one?

Please refer to the names listed on your invitation. If a plus-one has been reserved for you, you’ll be able to include them in your RSVP.

What about gifts?

Your presence is already a gift. If you still wish to bless us as we begin married life, a contribution to our future-home and honeymoon fund would be deeply appreciated.

Can we take photos during the ceremony?

We’re having an unplugged ceremony so everyone can be fully present. Please keep phones tucked away until after the recessional—our photo and video team have it covered.

When should we RSVP?

Please send your response by 10 January 2027 so we can finalize seating, meals, and other guest arrangements.
