Ang aming mga tradisyon

Wedding Traditions

Three rites will be woven into our mass — small, old gestures that Filipino families have carried through generations.

Veil Ceremony

Symbolizing the couple being clothed together in unity.

Cord Ceremony

A ceremonial cord placed around the couple, representing their lifelong bond.

Arrhae Ceremony

Thirteen coins symbolizing prosperity, responsibility, and the sharing of blessings.
