Dress code

Modern Filipiniana or formal garden attire.

We would love to see soft earth tones, deep greens, muted reds, and warm neutrals. Barong Tagalog, ternos, and contemporary Filipiniana details are warmly encouraged.

  • Ladies: Long or midi dresses, elegant jumpsuits, or modern Filipiniana.
  • Gentlemen: Barong Tagalog, suit, or formal long-sleeved attire with trousers.
  • Practical note: Some areas are lawn or stone; block heels or comfortable formal shoes are recommended.
  • Please reserve white, ivory, and bridal champagne for the bride.

Before you go

Helpful answers for our guests.

The details below are placeholders and can be personalized for each couple.

What time should I arrive?

Registration opens at 3:00 PM. Please arrive no later than 3:15 PM and be seated by 3:25 PM for the 3:30 PM ceremony.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Because the venue has a fixed capacity, seats are reserved for the names listed on the invitation. Your RSVP form will show the number of seats held for your party.

Are children invited?

We love your little ones. Unless a child is named on the invitation, however, the ceremony and reception will be an adults-only celebration.

Will the ceremony be unplugged?

Yes. Please keep phones and cameras away during the ceremony. Our photo team will capture everything, and you are welcome to take photos during cocktails and the reception.

What if I have allergies or dietary restrictions?

Please add them to your RSVP response. Our caterer will do their best to provide a suitable meal when informed before the RSVP deadline.

What is the weather usually like?

Tagaytay evenings in January can feel cool and breezy. Bring a light wrap or jacket, especially if you plan to enjoy the outdoor areas after sunset.

Who can I contact on the wedding day?

Please avoid calling the couple while they prepare. Contact our coordinator, Ana, at +63 917 123 4567 for urgent venue, transport, or accessibility questions.
