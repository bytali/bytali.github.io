Wedding website questions, answered clearly.

Useful details for couples comparing a digital wedding invitation, RSVP page, and guest information website in the Philippines.

What is a TaliPH wedding website?

It is a personalized, mobile-friendly digital invitation that brings your wedding details, RSVP form, schedule, venue maps, dress code, photos, registry information, and guest guidance into one shareable link.

How much is the Essential package?

The Essential wedding website package costs ₱3,990. Payment is 50% to begin the project and 50% upon completion before launch.

How long does delivery take?

The usual delivery time is 2–3 business days after the required wedding details and materials are provided.

What does the Essential package include?

It includes a premium landing page, your choice of available template, RSVP, wedding date and venue details, Google Maps, countdown, gallery, dress code, gift or registry section, mobile-friendly design, GitHub Pages hosting, and 7 days of free minor updates.

Does Essential include a custom domain?

Essential uses a GitHub Pages website address such as yourwedding.github.io. Custom domain connection is planned as part of the Premium package.

What should we send to get started?

Send your wedding date, venue details, photos, entourage information, RSVP questions, preferred wording, and visual references. TaliPH organizes these into a clear guest journey.

Can guests RSVP using a mobile phone?

Yes. Every TaliPH wedding website is mobile-friendly, and the RSVP form can collect attendance, plus-one details, meal preferences, and guest notes based on your setup.

Do you work with couples anywhere in the Philippines?

Yes. TaliPH works online with couples across the Philippines and can include Google Maps links for venues in any city or province.

Can we request changes after the website launches?

Yes. Essential includes 7 days of free minor updates after launch. One-year website maintenance is also available as an optional add-on.
